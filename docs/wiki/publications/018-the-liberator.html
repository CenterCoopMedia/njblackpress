Historical notes

No specific records were found for a publication called The Liberator published in Paterson, NJ around 1950 in the African American press context. This title should not be confused with William Lloyd Garrison's famous abolitionist newspaper The Liberator (Boston, 1831-1865), nor with the 1960s New York-based Liberator magazine (1961-1971) associated with the Black liberation movement. The Paterson Liberator may have been a small community newspaper serving the city's growing African American population during the postwar era. Physical records may exist at the Newark Public Library's New Jersey Information Center, the Passaic County Historical Society, or in the Danky & Hady national bibliography of African American newspapers and periodicals. A March 2026 search found no Library of Congress entry, no microfilm, and no records beyond its founding in Paterson in 1950. Its status is recorded as unknown on the Wikipedia list of African American newspapers in New Jersey.
