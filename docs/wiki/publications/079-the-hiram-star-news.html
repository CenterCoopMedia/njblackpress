Historical notes

No specific archival or historical records were found for The Hiram Star-News from Vauxhall, NJ in online databases. Vauxhall is an unincorporated community within Union Township, Union County, New Jersey. The 1947 start date places it in the immediate postwar period when African American communities in northern New Jersey were growing due to wartime migration and postwar economic opportunities. The name 'Hiram' may reference a fraternal lodge or community organization, as Hiram lodges were common in African American Freemasonry (Prince Hall Masonry). Physical records may exist at the Union Township Public Library, the Union County Historical Society, or in the Danky & Hady national bibliography. A March 2026 search found no library catalog entries, no microfilm holdings, and no digital records in any searched database.
