## Internet Archive: Newark African American newspapers

Collection: https://archive.org/details/newarkafamnewspapers
124 items. Newark Public Library. Public domain in the US per NPL rights note. Embed example:

`<iframe src="https://archive.org/embed/NewJerseyHeraldNews19390311" width="560" height="384" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen></iframe>`

Earliest listed issue: New Jersey Herald News, 21 May 1938 (`NewJerseyHeraldNews19380521a`).
Run continues through 1939, then 1941–1945. Identifiers alternate `NewarkHeraldYYYYMMDD` and `NewJerseyHeraldNewsYYYYMMDD`. This is a full-issue archive of the Herald / Herald News, not a mention clip.
