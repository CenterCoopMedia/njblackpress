## Historical notes

The Black Voice was published by the United Black Workers in Newark and served Black industrial workers, including those at Ford’s Mahwah plant. Volume 5, number 2, printed page 2, reports that the paper began in 1970, supporting the founding field. The undated volume 6, number 2, printed page 8, discusses events as occurring in 1976. That internal wording supports publication in 1976 or later and contradicts an exact 1975 end, but does not prove a 1976 cessation. Danky and Hady’s 1975? end and the Wisconsin union list’s holdings through June 1975 are uncertain catalog or holdings statements. The final year remains unknown. This title is distinct from Black Voice News in California and the Rutgers student publication Black Voice/Carta Boricua. The microfilm is held at Newark Public Library’s Charles F. Cummings New Jersey Information Center. Contact njreference@npl.org or 973-733-7775 for access. This paper is also distinct from The Black Voice at Syracuse University, a student publication founded in 1969.
