## Historical notes

The ALA Archives describes the Black Caucus Newsletter as serving readers since 1974. Danky and Hady, entry 795, page 77, instead describes a bimonthly serial beginning in 1971 and records Pomona, New Jersey, as its place of publication from September 1983 to April 1986. The earliest issue and the relationship between the two start dates remain unresolved. ALA Archives illustrates volume 13, number 6, from 1985–1986; this does not support a single semiannual schedule for the full run. BCALA’s newsletter page links issues through fall 2021. That page alone does not verify current activity; a recent issue is needed. The 1974 field retains the ALA Archives date pending that title-history review.
