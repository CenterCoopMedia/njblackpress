## Historical notes

The Jersey Express was a Montclair weekly published by Rumble Printing Service; Melvin B. Johnson is recorded as a previous editor. Danky and Hady, entry 3285, page 312, lists a Wisconsin Historical Society microfilm holding of volume 1, number 10, June 18, 1932. This corrects the earlier claim that no microfilm was found and establishes publication by that date. The bibliography gives an uncertain 1932? start and no exact end; neither an exact founding year nor the former 1940 cessation is established. The 1949 clippings about Johnson’s newspaper career do not establish this title’s lifespan. A May 24, 1932 Montclair Times item is a launch-date lead whose article text remains unread.
