## Mission statement

"New Jersey's only African-American-Hispanic news source.”.

## Key staff

Kamau Kujichagulia, publisher emeritus (April 2024 issue); earlier editor Pamela A. Sims.

## Historical notes

The Nubian News serves Trenton’s Black and Hispanic communities. Its February 5, 2021 issue dates its establishment to September 9, 1989. The July 31, 2022 issue announces the end of that print run, so uninterrupted print publication is not established. A publisher-hosted April 2024 joint issue bears The Nubian News and El Latino News names, states a print run of 5,000 copies, and names Kamau Kujichagulia as publisher emeritus. The later issue prevents treating 2022 as the title’s final publication year. The State Library’s 1989–2004 range describes historical holdings, not the full publication run. Digitized issues are available on the publisher’s website. Current activity and cadence in 2026 remain unverified; a recent issue is needed. Earlier archive research also recorded the podcast On The Case; its current schedule has not been checked.
