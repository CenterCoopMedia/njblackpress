## Historical notes

Published weekly in Camden by Camden News Publishing Co. Charles S. Boyer’s History of the press in Camden County, New Jersey (1921), pages 62–63, reports a first issue on May 8, 1915. The Directory of New Jersey newspapers, 1765–1970 (1977), page 37, gives the same start and leaves the end unknown. It lists Camden County Historical Society holdings dated May 22, June 26 and December 11, 1915, and March 9, 1918. The listed 1918 holding does not establish the final publication year. Boyer’s present-tense description in 1921 suggests possible later activity but does not establish a 1921 issue. The final publication year remains unknown; the title remains inactive. Current custody and the latest surviving issue need confirmation.
