## Historical notes

The Library of Congress, LCCN sn88071371, describes The Forum of Newark as 197?–???? and bases its description on volume 2, number 47, January 4, 1974. This establishes an issue described from 1974, not an exact 1970 launch or 1980 cessation. Both former exact years are unverified and are omitted. WorldCat OCLC 18514783 also leaves the end open. A possible merger with Essex Forum, record 42, remains unverified; separate catalog entries alone do not prove that relationship.
