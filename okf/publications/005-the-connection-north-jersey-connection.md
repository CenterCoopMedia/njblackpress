## Historical notes

The North Jersey Connection and its successor The Connection were Teaneck publications associated with Ralph F. Johnson. The Library of Congress describes North Jersey Connection, LCCN sn92060412, from an October 22, 1983 issue, and The Connection, LCCN sn88071369, from June 16, 1984. March 23, 1991 is the latter catalog’s latest issue consulted, not a final issue. Danky and Hady, entry 1791, page 172, records Wisconsin holdings beginning with volume 11, number 20, June 5, 1993, and gives an uncertain 1982 start. The holding contradicts the former 1991 end; neither an exact founding year nor a final year is established. The predecessor is described as biweekly and the later bibliography as weekly; schedules require dated mastheads. Records 5 and 131 share OCLC 18514803 and duplicate the successor identity. Both IDs are retained to preserve existing references, with the duplication explicit. An open catalog end does not establish present activity. The newspaper served Bergen County and northern New Jersey’s Black community, providing local reporting during and after Teaneck’s voluntary school desegregation in 1965.
